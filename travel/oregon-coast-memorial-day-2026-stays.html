Memorial Day Weekend Oregon Coast Stays (2026)

This report highlights top lodging options for a relaxing Memorial Day weekend (May 22-25, 2026) for couples, focusing on the central and southern Oregon coast.

Overview

The central and southern Oregon coast offers a more tranquil alternative to the busier northern towns (like Cannon Beach). Towns such as Yachats, Gold Beach, and Port Orford are particularly known for their rugged beauty and quiet atmosphere.

This page matters because it reflects Shane's preference for lower-friction, quieter, higher-signal travel choices over crowded default picks.

---

Top 12 Lodging Options

1. Wildspring Guest Habitat (Port Orford)

  • Type: Secluded Cabin Resort (Adults Only)
  • Nightly Range: $350 - $550
  • Pros: Incredible privacy, 5-acre forest habitat, open-air slate hot tub, eco-luxury focus.
  • Cons: Remote location; limited nearby dining options.
  • Booking: wildspring.com

2. Overleaf Lodge & Spa (Yachats)

  • Type: Oceanfront Resort & Spa
  • Nightly Range: $400 - $650
  • Pros: World-class spa, direct ocean views from every room, soaking tubs, very relaxing vibe.
  • Cons: Popular holiday destination; requires early booking.
  • Booking: overleaflodge.com

3. Tu Tu Tun Lodge (Gold Beach)

  • Type: Riverside Luxury Lodge
  • Nightly Range: $450 - $800
  • Pros: Exceptional personalized service, riverside wood-burning sauna, adult-oriented.
  • Cons: Not directly on the beach (located on the Rogue River).
  • Booking: tututun.com

4. Heceta Lighthouse Bed and Breakfast (Florence)

  • Type: Historic B&B (Lighthouse Keeper's House)
  • Nightly Range: $350 - $550
  • Pros: Unique historic stay, famous 7-course gourmet breakfast, stunning secluded cliffs.
  • Cons: Shared common areas; limited number of rooms available.
  • Booking: hecetalighthouse.com

5. Endicott Gardens Bed & Breakfast (Gold Beach)

  • Type: Botanical B&B and Spa (Adults Only)
  • Nightly Range: $250 - $450
  • Pros: Quiet botanical garden setting, on-site spa, refined agriturismo feel.
  • Cons: A short drive to the ocean rather than beachfront.
  • Booking: endicottgardens.com

6. Ocean House (Newport)

  • Type: Boutique Adult-Focused B&B
  • Nightly Range: $300 - $500
  • Pros: Direct access to Agate Beach, wine and beer social hour, very romantic.
  • Cons: Newport town center can be busy over holiday weekends.
  • Booking: oceanhouse.com

7. Adobe Resort (Yachats)

  • Type: Full-Service Oceanfront Resort
  • Nightly Range: $250 - $450
  • Pros: Direct beach access, oceanfront dining, every room has a view.
  • Cons: More family-friendly/busy than the secluded B&Bs.
  • Booking: adoberesort.com

8. Bay Point Landing (Coos Bay)

  • Type: Modern Cabins & Airstreams
  • Nightly Range: $200 - $400
  • Pros: Modern architectural design, waterfront property, indoor pool.
  • Cons: Coos Bay has a more industrial feel compared to scenic Yachats or Bandon.
  • Booking: baypointlanding.com

9. The Wildflower Inn (Gold Beach)

  • Type: Boutique Hotel
  • Nightly Range: $200 - $350
  • Pros: Stylish modern decor, deep soaking tubs, great value for luxury.
  • Cons: Short drive to the beach; lacks on-site restaurant.
  • Booking: thewildflowerinn.com

10. Inn at Face Rock (Bandon)

  • Type: Oceanfront Inn
  • Nightly Range: $250 - $450
  • Pros: Walkable beach access, quiet and personalized service, near Bandon's Old Town.
  • Cons: Can be extremely windy due to the rugged coastline.
  • Booking: innatfacerock.com

11. Ocean Suites Motel (Brookings)

  • Type: Spacious Suite Motel
  • Nightly Range: $180 - $300
  • Pros: Quiet location, larger suites with kitchenettes, good value.
  • Cons: More of a traditional motel feel; lacks luxury resort amenities.
  • Booking: oceansuitesmotel.com

12. Chetco River Inn (Brookings)

  • Type: Riverfront Inn
  • Nightly Range: $200 - $350
  • Pros: Off-the-beaten-path, very peaceful river setting, "old school" charm.
  • Cons: Further from town and beach; older facilities.
  • Booking: chetcoriverinn.com

---

Recommended: 5 Best Picks for Quiet & Relaxation

If relaxation is your absolute priority for Memorial Day 2026, these are our top 5 recommendations:

  1. Wildspring Guest Habitat (Port Orford): The ultimate for couples seeking complete seclusion and a connection with nature.
  2. Overleaf Lodge & Spa (Yachats): Best for those who want to alternate between spa treatments and watching the waves crash from their balcony.
  3. Tu Tu Tun Lodge (Gold Beach): Best for high-end service and a tranquil, upscale riverside escape.
  4. Heceta Lighthouse B&B (Florence): Best for a romantic, historic experience with a legendary morning breakfast.
  5. Endicott Gardens B&B (Gold Beach): Best for a refined, quiet garden retreat away from the salt spray and crowds.

--- Note: Prices are estimates for the 2026 Memorial Day holiday weekend based on historical trends. Booking 6-12 months in advance is highly recommended.
