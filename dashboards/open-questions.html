Open Questions

Unresolved questions across Shane's lanes. These are not philosophical — each one is specific enough that an answer would change a real decision. If a question has been answered, it should be deleted from this page (and the answer captured wherever the decision actually lives).

Chickens, OpenCluck, borrowed land

  1. Power for the OpenCluck device: solar + 18650 battery pack vs. running a wire from the existing structure. Which one survives a wet Oregon winter without manual intervention? See OpenCluck — Chicken Coop IoT Monitor.
  2. WiFi range from the house to the coop — is it usable, or does this need a dedicated WiFi extender or LTE backup before Phase 1 deploys?
  3. MQ-135 placement: ammonia pools at floor level where hens sleep, but a floor-mounted sensor will get fouled fast. What is the right height/protection compromise?
  4. Should the flock get supplemental winter lighting? It extends the productive season but shortens hen lifespan — what's the right call for borrowed-land economics? See Backyard Chickens — Oregon Keeper Notes.
  5. Is the borrowed-land arrangement durable enough to justify Phase 2 (egg-count camera)? Or should every investment stay portable?

Hot sauce vending machine

  1. Is the hot sauce vending machine actually viable in winter at outdoor pods, or is it a 6-month seasonal play? Portland pods lose 50%+ foot traffic November through March. See Gresham Vending Ops — Running a Machine in East Portland.
  2. Does the friend supplying hot sauce want to be involved operationally, or pure product supply? This shapes whether it's a wholesale model or a consignment split. See Hot Sauce Vending Machine.
  3. Buy used vs. lease vs. build custom for the first machine — what's the lowest-regret choice given that placement isn't locked in yet?
  4. LLC and insurance — required before placing on someone else's property, but at what level? A single machine probably doesn't need a full commercial policy.
  5. Is the Stripe Verifone UX700 actually available and shippable, or is the fallback attended tablet + reader the realistic Day 1 path?

El Salvador and travel

  1. When is the next El Salvador trip? The future-trip program is structured as coastal + mountain + marine blocks but no dates are set. See Future El Salvador trip program.
  2. Should the next trip be timed to whale season (Oct–Feb) at Los Cobanos, or to a Bitcoin community event? Both are valid but optimize different lanes.
  3. Is there a structured retro from the previous trip worth writing? The raw corpus in Shane San Salvador trip corpus hasn't been promoted into a travel/ retro page.
  4. Is operator site rescue a real El Salvador business lane Shane will pursue, or just a documented possibility? See Operator site rescue (first business).

Bitcoin local commerce

  1. Are there actually Lightning-accepting merchants in Gresham and Happy Valley, or is Portland proper the only realistic spend zone? The merchant table in Bitcoin Circular Economy — Portland / Gresham / Happy Valley still has TBDs in those rows.
  2. What's the right outreach script for asking a non-Bitcoin merchant to add BTCPay? Is there a version that works for restaurants vs. retail vs. service businesses?
  3. Should there be a separate kids-oriented spend node (toy capsule machine) before or after the adult hot sauce machine ships? See Kids Sats Toy Vending.

AI agent stack and Shanepedia itself

  1. Should the daily random spark Telegram loop run as a cron, or as a manual trigger during the morning routine? The contract is documented in Daily Random Spark Loop but not wired up yet.
  2. Is the public site's noindex, nofollow posture the right long-term call, or is there a future where parts of Shanepedia are deliberately SEO-visible?
  3. How aggressively should the wiki be pruned? The page count is at 76 and growing — at what point does the index get too noisy for both humans and agents to navigate?
  4. Should there be more parent-level synthesis pages (like the new dashboards), or does that just create more surface area to maintain?
