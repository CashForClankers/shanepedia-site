WhatsApp booking funnels

WhatsApp is the primary booking channel for small tourism operators in El Salvador. Most operators run their entire business through WhatsApp but have no structured funnel. This is a concrete, sellable service.

Current operator pattern

  1. Tourist finds operator through word of mouth or a random Google listing
  2. Tourist sends WhatsApp message: "do you do tours?"
  3. Operator replies with price and availability (sometimes hours later)
  4. Back-and-forth about dates, group size, pickup location
  5. Booking confirmed via text, payment on arrival (cash or sometimes Bitcoin)

Problems with this pattern

  • No after-hours response — lost leads while operator sleeps
  • No pre-built responses for common questions
  • No photos, itineraries, or social proof in the chat flow
  • No follow-up or review request after the experience
  • No way to handle English-speaking tourists if operator speaks limited English
  • No upsell or cross-sell of related experiences

Better funnel design

Stage 1: Landing page with WhatsApp CTA

  • Simple single-page site with: hero photo, what you get, price, seasonal info, WhatsApp button
  • Button pre-fills a message: "Hi, I'd like to book [experience] for [date]"
  • Page loads in under 2 seconds, works on any phone

Stage 2: WhatsApp Business quick replies

  • Set up WhatsApp Business (free) with:
  • Greeting message with key info
  • Quick reply templates for pricing, availability, directions
  • Photo catalog of experiences
  • Bilingual responses (Spanish + English)

Stage 3: Follow-up automation

  • After experience: send thank-you + review request
  • Before peak season: send reminder to past customers
  • Use WhatsApp broadcast lists (not groups) for announcements

Pricing this as a service

  • Basic site + WhatsApp setup: $150-300 one-time
  • Monthly management (respond to English leads, post updates): $50-100/mo
  • Full funnel build (site + WhatsApp Business + templates + photo shoot): $500

First targets

  • Victor at Los Cobanos (whale watching, reef snorkeling)
  • Coffee finca operators on Ruta de las Flores
  • Surf instructors at El Tunco and El Zonte
  • Boutique hotel/hostel owners without proper booking flows

Agent automation opportunity

Agents can:

  • Generate landing page copy from operator photos and descriptions
  • Create bilingual WhatsApp quick-reply templates
  • Monitor and flag unanswered WhatsApp leads
  • Draft follow-up messages and seasonal announcements
  • A/B test different CTA copy on landing pages
