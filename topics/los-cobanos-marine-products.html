Los Cobanos marine products

Los Cobanos is the strongest single-location marine tourism anchor in El Salvador. It has coral reefs, whale watching, local guide infrastructure, and growing international interest. The products here are seasonal and tide-dependent, which creates a natural scarcity that improves pricing power.

Current offerings

Reef snorkeling

  • Volcanic rocks and coral reefs host diverse fish and sea life
  • Available year-round (tide-dependent)
  • Low cost, accessible to beginners

Whale watching

  • Humpback whales: October to February (peak Dec-March)
  • 43 MARN-authorized local guides
  • Growing fast — "a lot of humpback whale tourism in the last 2-3 years"
  • Boats depart with captain briefing on area, weather, route

Marine fauna boat tours

  • Dolphins, sea turtles, rays year-round
  • Listed on GetYourGuide as "incredible marine wildlife watching"
  • Combines well with snorkeling for half-day packages

Product opportunities

1. Whale season calendar product

  • Pre-bookable whale watching blocks for Oct-Feb season
  • Early-bird pricing for international visitors
  • WhatsApp reminder sequence as season approaches
  • "Whale season is open" broadcast to past visitors

2. Tide-aware snorkeling scheduler

  • Simple tool that shows best snorkeling windows based on tide tables
  • Reduces cancellations and bad experiences
  • Differentiator: "we only take you when conditions are right"

3. Marine + highland combo package

  • Day 1: Los Cobanos reef snorkel + sunset
  • Day 2: Transfer to highlands, coffee tour + hot springs
  • Day 3: Birding morning at finca, return to coast or San Salvador
  • No one sells this east-west combo as a single product

4. Victor partnership formalization

  • Victor is the key local contact — whale watching and reef guide
  • Propose: landing page, WhatsApp funnel, English booking flow
  • Revenue share or flat monthly fee for digital services
  • Document the partnership model for replication with other operators

Competitive landscape

  • GetYourGuide lists Los Cobanos marine tours
  • Viator has a few options but thin inventory
  • No operator has their own strong English-language web presence
  • The authorized guide network (43 people) is an underexploited asset

Next steps

  1. Contact Victor about landing page + WhatsApp funnel pilot
  2. Build a whale-season landing page before October 2026
  3. Create tide-calendar integration for snorkeling scheduling
  4. Draft the marine + highland combo itinerary
  5. Photo-document Los Cobanos marine experiences on next trip
