Bitcoin commerce map

ElSalKB contains 1,092 verified Bitcoin-accepting businesses across El Salvador. This is a massive underexploited dataset for building Bitcoin tourism products.

Concentration by area

| Area | Bitcoin businesses | |------|-------------------| | Broader El Salvador | 713 | | San Salvador | 80 | | Berlín | 49 | | El Tunco | 28 | | Santa Ana | 21 | | El Zonte | 15 | | La Libertad | 13 | | Antiguo Cuscatlán | 11 | | Concepción de Ataco | 8 | | Santa Tecla | 7 |

Notable Bitcoin venues by tourism corridor

El Zonte (Bitcoin Beach)

  • Bitcoin Beach Office, Cafe Cocoa, Cherito Cafe, Eco Suites, El Chango, Goodlife Real Estate, La Canasta Mini Market, Lapalapa Restaurant, Clinica Dental El Zonte

El Tunco (surf corridor)

  • Acantilados, Balance Hotel Surf & Yoga, Canelos Microbus Tours, Canuck's Guest House, Capt. Cook Restaurant, The Duck Dive, Garage Burgers, HZO, Kitsune Tattoo Studio

Ruta de las Flores (highlands)

  • Ataco: Axul Coffee Artesanías, Blossom Bubble Tea, Café San Leonardo, Casa 1800, Hotel Don Quijote
  • Apaneca: Cibernautica, Ecole Café & Bistró, Flores de Apaneca
  • Juayúa: Cellshop, Dónde Ata & Gaby

Product opportunities from this data

1. Bitcoin walking tour guides

  • Create self-guided walking tour maps for El Zonte, El Tunco, and Ataco
  • Show which businesses accept Lightning — tourists can "spend sats" at each stop
  • Gamify: "Visit 10 Bitcoin businesses on the Ruta de las Flores"

2. Bitcoin-friendly accommodation directory

  • Curate a list of BTC-accepting hotels, hostels, and guesthouses
  • Add to WhatsApp booking funnel: "Pay with Bitcoin — no conversion needed"
  • Target crypto conferences and Bitcoin meetups for promotion

3. Bitcoin business verification service

  • Many listings may be outdated — verify which businesses still accept BTC
  • Offer verification badge + listing on a curated directory site
  • Small monthly fee for premium listing with photos and booking link

4. Highland Bitcoin trail

  • Nobody markets Ataco/Apaneca/Juayúa as a Bitcoin travel corridor
  • Combine with coffee tours and finca stays: "Pay for your coffee tour in sats"
  • Differentiation: most Bitcoin tourism content focuses only on El Zonte

Data source

All Bitcoin business data comes from the ElSalKB experiences database, sourced from Bitcoin payment node maps and OpenStreetMap. The data needs periodic re-verification to ensure businesses still accept Lightning payments.
