Agentic business studio

Shanepedia should function like a founder studio that continuously turns research, travel, photos, relationships, and design taste into lightweight businesses. The important move is not one giant startup. It is a portfolio of small, agent-assisted offers that can compound.

High-fit business lanes

Current status: active thesis

Next action: pressure-test which lane can ship proof and get paid first

  1. Operator site rescue: simple landing-page rebuilds, copy rewrites, and WhatsApp-first booking flows for small businesses.
  2. Founder research dossiers: recurring market, place, and relationship briefs for founders, travelers, or local operators.
  3. Agent-ready knowledge systems: parent wiki setups for people with messy projects, travel plans, networks, and ideas.
  4. Photo-to-offer intelligence: turn image streams into business ideas, product critiques, inspiration boards, and follow-up tasks.
  5. Travel intelligence products: curated place/operator briefings, route plans, and niche local trust maps.
  6. Micro-site factory: launch small focused sites for one offer, one market, or one relationship lane at a time.
  7. Local business AI enablement: practical automations, intake tools, and follow-up systems that avoid enterprise overhead.
  8. Network and partnership graphing: turn people, intros, and repeated contexts into a searchable founder relationship system.
  9. Founder OS as a service: productize Shanepedia itself as an agent-readable founder command center.
  10. Instant-boot brand systems: sell ultra-fast founder and operator sites that communicate clarity and competence immediately.
  11. Micro-SaaS operator toolkit: ship small utilities for booking, payments, copy translation, and lead capture instead of one bloated platform.
  12. Creator enablement for domain experts: help guides, hosts, and niche experts package what they know into products and funnels.

Likely first buyers

  • independent operators who need a better site or funnel this week
  • founders who want a cleaner research and memory system
  • local businesses that need simple automation without software sprawl
  • experts with real knowledge and weak packaging

Product rule

One of the strongest productizable forms of this studio is the LLM-maintained founder wiki itself: a system where source ingest, synthesis, dashboards, and agent-readable memory compound over time.

Each lane should answer:

  • who pays first?
  • what can agents automate immediately?
  • what proof can be published quickly?
  • what founder advantage does Shanepedia make durable?
