Agentic business studio

Shanepedia should function like a founder studio that continuously turns research, travel, photos, relationships, and design taste into lightweight businesses. The important move is not one giant startup. It is a portfolio of small, agent-assisted offers that can compound.

High-fit business lanes

Current status: active thesis

Next action: pressure-test which lane can ship proof and get paid first

  1. Operator site rescue: simple landing-page rebuilds, copy rewrites, and WhatsApp-first booking flows for small businesses.
  2. Founder research dossiers: recurring market, place, and relationship briefs for founders, travelers, or local operators.
  3. Agent-ready knowledge systems: parent wiki setups for people with messy projects, travel plans, networks, and ideas.
  4. Photo-to-offer intelligence: turn image streams into business ideas, product critiques, inspiration boards, and follow-up tasks.
  5. Travel intelligence products: curated place/operator briefings, route plans, and niche local trust maps.
  6. Micro-site factory: launch small focused sites for one offer, one market, or one relationship lane at a time.
  7. Local business AI enablement: practical automations, intake tools, and follow-up systems that avoid enterprise overhead.
  8. Network and partnership graphing: turn people, intros, and repeated contexts into a searchable founder relationship system.
  9. Founder OS as a service: productize Shanepedia itself as an agent-readable founder command center.
  10. Instant-boot brand systems: sell ultra-fast founder and operator sites that communicate clarity and competence immediately.
  11. Micro-SaaS operator toolkit: ship small utilities for booking, payments, copy translation, and lead capture instead of one bloated platform.
  12. Creator enablement for domain experts: help guides, hosts, and niche experts package what they know into products and funnels.

Likely first buyers

  • independent operators who need a better site or funnel this week
  • founders who want a cleaner research and memory system
  • local businesses that need simple automation without software sprawl
  • experts with real knowledge and weak packaging

Product rule

One of the strongest productizable forms of this studio is the LLM-maintained founder wiki itself: a system where source ingest, synthesis, dashboards, and agent-readable memory compound over time.

Each lane should answer:

  • who pays first?
  • what can agents automate immediately?
  • what proof can be published quickly?
  • what founder advantage does Shanepedia make durable?

Parent role

This page is the portfolio map. Specific projects such as ElSalKB, Los Cobanos work, or future location lanes should plug into it rather than replace it.

Business seed: Operator Signal Desk

  • A monthly service for El Salvador operators and remote founders who need one calm, high-signal page plus an agent-managed follow-up loop.
  • Package includes fast landing-page cleanup, WhatsApp-first intake copy, weekly inquiry summaries, and a small opportunity brief for partnerships or founder traffic.
  • Fits Shane's edge of simple sites that boot instantly, practical operator empathy, and agent-assisted synthesis instead of bloated software.
  • First likely buyers are small operators with weak packaging and founders running retreats, scouting partnerships, or sending people into trusted local experiences.
  • Could start as a done-for-you service, then harden into reusable templates and agent workflows across operator-site rescue and micro-site factory lanes.

Business seed: Los Cobanos Booking Friction Scan

  • A paid intelligence brief for Los Cobanos operators, hotels, or guides that flags where trust, booking clarity, bilingual copy, and WhatsApp response flow are leaking revenue.
  • Uses Shane's instant-boot UX taste plus agentic teardown workflows to turn a site, menu, flyer, or chat transcript into a concise fix list with suggested copy.
  • Best first buyers are marine and tourism operators who already have demand but weak packaging, especially where whale watching, snorkeling, or local hotel referrals depend on fast trust.
  • Can be sold as a one-off scan, then expanded into done-for-you page cleanup, reply templates, and monthly booking funnel care.
  • Creates proof fast because each brief can become before/after portfolio material for the broader operator site rescue lane.

Business seed: Whale Window Reply Desk

  • A managed bilingual WhatsApp reply service for Los Cobanos whale-watching operators, hotels, and guides who currently leak bookings through slow or unclear chat handling.
  • Claude/Gemini/Codex can draft replies, summarize inquiries, flag hot leads, and turn repeated questions into cleaner offer copy and deposit instructions.
  • It directly fits the tourism-landscape gap that whale watching is booming, English-language packaging is weak, and no agent-automated booking intelligence layer exists yet.
  • Shane's edge is the combination of instant-trust UX, high-signal synthesis, and practical operator empathy rather than building bloated booking software.
  • First proof can come from one live inbox mockup and a one-page offer sent to a Los Cobanos contact or partner hotel.

Business seed: Victor Deposit Clarity Bot

  • A lightweight agent-managed WhatsApp layer for Los Cobanos marine operators that turns "is this available?" chats into clear bilingual deposit instructions and confirmed bookings.
  • Product fits Shane's low-friction UX taste: short reply templates, obvious next step, and trust-first copy instead of long sales pages.
  • Claude/Gemini/Codex can draft replies, summarize lead status daily, and auto-generate FAQ snippets that feed back into operator pages.
  • First buyers are operators like Victor and nearby partner hotels already getting demand but losing conversions at payment/confidence handoff.
  • Start as done-for-you inbox management, then expand into a reusable operator onboarding kit across the local business AI enablement lane.
