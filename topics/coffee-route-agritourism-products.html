Coffee route agritourism products

The Ruta de las Flores highlands have strong agritourism infrastructure but weak packaging. Individual fincas offer excellent experiences — the opportunity is in curating and connecting them into coherent arcs.

Key fincas and experiences

Finca El Carmen (Ataco)

  • 4+ generations of traditional coffee production
  • Tour includes harvesting, roasting, and tasting
  • Original 1930 building "La Casona" as tasting venue
  • Strong heritage story for content

Finca La Esperanza (Santa Ana volcano slope)

  • 4-hour tour through volcano-slope coffee forest
  • Strong birding and flower biodiversity
  • Mountain views as selling point

Finca Santa Leticia (Juayua)

  • Boutique lodging + coffee tour combination
  • Archaeological artifacts on property
  • Birding destination

Termales de Santa Teresa

  • Hot springs near Ataco
  • Natural complement to coffee tours
  • Relaxation anchor for multi-day itineraries

Existing tour pricing

  • Full-day private tours from San Salvador: $125/person (7-8 hours)
  • Includes transport, 4 colonial towns, and one coffee experience
  • Most tours sold through Viator, GetYourGuide, TripAdvisor

Product gaps Shane can fill

  1. Multi-day highland arc: No one packages a 2-3 day route covering coffee, birding, hot springs, and finca stays as a single product
  2. Direct-to-finca booking: Most fincas don't have English booking pages — tourists go through middlemen (Viator takes 20-30% commission)
  3. Bitcoin payment onboarding: None of these fincas accept Bitcoin despite being in a Bitcoin-legal-tender country
  4. Content and storytelling: The heritage stories (La Casona, multi-generational farming) are underexploited in marketing
  5. Birding niche: Los Naranjos and highland fincas have serious birding potential but no dedicated birding tourism product

Actionable next steps

  1. Visit 3-4 fincas on next trip and document with photos
  2. Propose WhatsApp funnel + landing page to 1-2 finca owners
  3. Draft a "Highland Arc" itinerary page for the Shanepedia public layer
  4. Research birding species lists for content differentiation
  5. Price out a 2-night highland package (finca stay + coffee + hot springs + birding)
