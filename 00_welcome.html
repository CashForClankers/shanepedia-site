Welcome to Shanepedia

Shanepedia is the parent founder/operator wiki for Shane Neeley — life, businesses, projects, relationships, trips, history, and ongoing research. It is hand-maintained as a compiled markdown layer that agents and humans can both read.

If you want a fast read of what's actually alive right now, start with the three dashboards below.
